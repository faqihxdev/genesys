# Persona Configurations for New Year Sales Campaign

This document provides four different persona configurations that can be used to test the New Year Sales funnels. Each configuration targets a different customer segment with specific constraints.

## Persona Config 1: Holiday Bargain Hunters

```
{
  name: "Holiday Bargain Hunters",
  description: "Price-sensitive shoppers actively looking for the best New Year deals and discounts",
  projectId: "proj-newyearsales",
  type: "custom",
  size: 100,
  constraints: "These personas should be price-conscious consumers from diverse backgrounds who are motivated primarily by savings. They are typically comparison shoppers who check multiple stores before making a purchase. They respond strongly to limited-time offers, flash sales, and discount codes. Many wait specifically for holiday sales like New Year to make their larger purchases. They have varying income levels but share a common trait of stretching their budget through strategic shopping. Some are buying necessities at a discount, while others are looking to splurge on luxury items they wouldn't normally purchase without a significant discount. They heavily research products before buying and are less brand-loyal than convenience or quality-focused customers."
}
```

## Persona Config 2: New Year Resolution Makers

```
{
  name: "New Year Resolution Makers",
  description: "Customers motivated by self-improvement goals and fresh starts associated with the New Year",
  projectId: "proj-newyearsales",
  type: "custom",
  size: 75,
  constraints: "These personas are motivated by New Year's resolutions and personal transformation goals. They're looking for products that support fitness, health, organization, learning, or other self-improvement areas. They're less price-sensitive and more feature/benefit-focused, concerned with how products will help them achieve their goals. They respond well to aspirational marketing and educational content that positions products as solutions to their resolution challenges. They're more likely to be first-time buyers in certain categories (fitness equipment, organizational tools, educational courses) and may require more guidance in their purchase decisions. They have a higher tendency to abandon carts or delay purchases if they become overwhelmed with choices or information. They're receptive to bundles or starter packs that simplify decision-making and provide a complete solution to their goals."
}
```

## Persona Config 3: Loyal Brand Customers

```
{
  name: "Loyal Brand Customers",
  description: "Existing customers with previous purchase history who engage regularly with the brand",
  projectId: "proj-newyearsales",
  type: "custom",
  size: 50,
  constraints: "These personas represent your existing customer base with varying loyalty history. They've made at least one purchase in the past year and have some familiarity with your products and services. They range from occasional shoppers to brand advocates. They respond well to personalized recommendations based on past purchases and loyalty status recognition. They appreciate early access to sales and exclusive offers that make them feel valued. Their purchasing decisions are influenced by previous experiences with the brand and the loyalty benefits offered. They're interested in complementary products to ones they already own and upgrades to their existing items. They're more likely to engage with referral programs and share offers with friends. They have varying price sensitivity but generally have higher average order values than new customers. They expect a seamless shopping experience and quick resolution to any issues, having higher expectations due to their established relationship with the brand."
}
```

## Persona Config 4: General Public

```
{
  name: "General Public",
  description: "Diverse cross-section of potential customers with varying levels of purchase intent during the New Year period",
  projectId: "proj-newyearsales",
  type: "custom",
  size: 150,
  constraints: "These personas represent a broad cross-section of the general public with widely varying demographics, psychographics, and shopping behaviors. They include casual browsers with no specific purchase intent, gift-seekers shopping for others, people with mild interest in New Year deals but not actively hunting for bargains, and individuals who stumbled upon the site through various channels. They have diverse income levels, technical abilities, and shopping preferences. Their knowledge of products ranges from expert to complete novice. Some are impulse buyers while others are methodical decision-makers. They have varying levels of price sensitivity, brand awareness, and time constraints. Some are shopping on mobile devices in brief moments of free time, while others are carefully researching on desktops. This group serves as a control group that represents the average visitor rather than a targeted segment, allowing for discovery of unexpected patterns and opportunities that might be missed when focusing only on predefined customer segments."
}
```

## Potential Use Cases

1. **Testing Funnel Effectiveness**: Run each persona configuration through each of the three funnels to identify which funnel works best for which customer segment.

2. **Conversion Rate Optimization**: Identify stages where specific persona types are dropping off and optimize those touchpoints.

3. **Content Refinement**: Analyze which messaging resonates most strongly with each persona type to refine marketing copy.

4. **Pricing Strategy Testing**: Compare how different discount structures and offers perform across the persona segments.

5. **Loyalty Program Enhancement**: Use the "Loyal Brand Customers" persona responses to improve retention strategies and loyalty benefits.

6. **General Appeal Assessment**: Use the "General Public" persona group to evaluate how your funnels perform with random traffic and identify unexpected opportunities or barriers.

## Implementation Notes

When generating personas from these configurations, the constraints will guide the creation of diverse but realistic customer profiles within each segment. These personas can then be simulated through the New Year Sales funnels to predict behavior patterns and identify optimization opportunities.

The varying size parameters (100, 75, 50, and 150) provide different sample sizes for statistical analysis while keeping the total number of personas manageable for simulation purposes. The General Public configuration has the largest sample size to ensure sufficient diversity in this broad category.
